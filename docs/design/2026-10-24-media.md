# Media: photographs, documents, sound and film beside a person

**Status:** proposal from the steps session to the site-redesign session, 24 October 2026; not built. The owner asked,
in their own words: "Can the person design have some space for photos if available or other media, optional, but
properly mapped in the data model, and not slowing down the site while storing full quality somewhere". The page is the
redesign session's; the model is this repository's. It builds on
[sources and citations](2026-10-24-sources-and-citations.md).

## 1. What the standards say (read from `standards/rico-1.1-axioms.json`, 24 October 2026)

- A photograph, a letter or a recorded testimony is a **record** (`rico:Record`): a source in our registry. What we
  store and show is an **instantiation** of it (`rico:Instantiation`, linked by `rico:hasOrHadInstantiation`, domain
  `RecordResource`, range `Instantiation`): a scan, a digital file, a recording.
- What a record shows or speaks of is its subject: `rico:hasOrHadSubject` (domain `RecordResource`, range `Thing`): a
  person, a place, an event.
- Terms of use and access: `rico:conditionsOfUse`, `rico:conditionsOfAccess` (domain `Instantiation` or
  `RecordResource`, a literal).
- Size: `rico:width`, `rico:height` (domain `Thing`).
- EHRI 0.2.0 adds nothing for media; RiC-O's terms stand. No new vocabulary type is needed for the first build: a
  medium's kind is a closed local list with a reason (§2), mapped to `rico:hasContentOfType` on the record once its
  values are read on RiC-O's own ContentType list (not yet read; said so).

## 2. The shape

**The record** is a `Source` (kind `photograph`, `family_document`, `oral_history`, or the archive's own), with its
holder, as the sources model has it. New on the source: `subjects`, the ids of the persons, places or events it shows
or speaks of (`rico:hasOrHadSubject`).

**A medium** (new part of the shared record, `media`), one instantiation we hold or link to:

| field | meaning | public | maps to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | stable, address-ready | yes | — |
| `source_id` | the record it instantiates | yes | `rico:isOrWasInstantiationOf` |
| `kind` | `image`, `audio`, `video`, `document` (local, with its reason) | yes | — |
| `caption` | ours, reviewed, for the page | yes | — |
| `alt` | ours, what the picture shows, for a screen reader (images only, required) | yes | — |
| `width`, `height`, `duration` | of the master, so the page reserves the space before it loads | yes | `rico:width`, `rico:height` |
| `conditions_of_use` | the holder's terms, as written | yes | `rico:conditionsOfUse` |
| `rights` | a rightsstatements.org or Creative Commons URI, where one applies | yes | — |
| `cleared` | `{by, on}`: the owner's word that it may be shown; nothing from the family's papers or a privately obtained copy is shown without it | yes (that it is cleared) | — |
| `link` | for audio or video not cleared: the holder's own page (Shoah Foundation, USHMM), on its host | yes | — |
| `master` | `{key, sha256, mime, bytes}`: the full-quality file in private storage | **no** | — |
| `derived` | the widths made for the site (the page pictures' `-w400`, `-w800`, `-w1200`, `-w100`), WebP | yes | — |

**Storage.** The master is kept at full quality in a private bucket that is never served (a second R2 bucket beside
`pruzhany-page-pictures`, or a private prefix the public domain cannot reach), named by its SHA-256. The writer makes the
WebP widths from it, as `site_pictures` makes the page pictures' (`SMALLER` here, `REMOTE_WIDTHS` on the site: one list),
and puts only those on `pictures.pruzhanychronicles.org`. The page gets `width` and `height` with the data, so nothing
moves as it loads; images load lazily below the first screen. A medium that is not `cleared` is never derived or served:
the page says that a photograph exists, held by whom, and nothing more.

**IIIF.** Not now. When deep zoom is wanted for a medium (as for the page pictures), a IIIF image service over the
private master is the standard way; `derived` then gains its info.json address.

## 3. Checks

- `source_id` names a registered source; every `subjects` id names a person, place or event.
- An `image` has `alt`; every medium has `width` and `height` (or `duration`) once derived.
- `derived` or a served address only with `cleared`; `master` never leaves the private record (a leak test on the site).
- `link` is on its holder's host.

## 4. Open for the redesign session and the council

- Whether captions and alt texts are written by a session and reviewed, or by the owner.
- Whether the family's photographs go through the owner's batch one by one (`cleared` per medium) or per collection.
- Where on the person page media sit (the page's), and whether the people index shows a portrait.
